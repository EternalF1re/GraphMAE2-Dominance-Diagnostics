# Decision and Reporting Rules

The following checks were used for the later reference estimates:

- all component-gradient ratios must be finite and positive;
- all four degree groups must be represented;
- the bootstrap coefficient-interval ratio must not exceed 1.5;
- the two panel estimates must differ by no more than a factor of 1.25; and
- saved-checkpoint medians must differ by no more than a factor of 2.

**Provenance status:** these checks were applied/formalized during later
read-only adjudication. They must not be described as universally fixed before
the earliest diagnostic-row collection.

The package reports authoritative Arxiv and Reddit reference estimates from
the final frozen evidence set. It does not expose a universal dominance
threshold and does not characterize the practical sampling design as optimal or
unbiased.
